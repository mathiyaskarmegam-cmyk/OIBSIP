# Task 4 – Infographic

## Oasis Infobyte Graphic Design Internship

### Title
**THE POWER OF COLOUR**

### Objective
Create a visually compelling infographic presenting information about colour psychology, emotions, perception, and behaviour.

## Topic
Colour Psychology in Design

The infographic explores how different colours can communicate emotions, influence perception, and contribute to visual communication and branding.

## Sections
- Red – Energy & Attention
- Blue – Trust & Calm
- Yellow – Happiness & Optimism
- Green – Nature & Balance
- Purple – Creativity & Luxury
- Black – Power & Sophistication
- Choosing Colour With Purpose

## Tools Used
- Figma

## Design Features
- Clear title and subtitle
- Multiple informational sections
- Colour-based visual elements
- Icons
- Data visualization
- Process diagram
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Top-to-bottom visual flow
- Consistent design system
- Sources section

## Dimensions
800 × 2000 px

## Colour Palette
The infographic uses colour as the primary visual language while maintaining a consistent dark editorial background and controlled accent colours.

## Output
High-resolution PNG
